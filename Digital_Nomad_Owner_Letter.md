# Remote-Work / Ownership Letter for the Digital Nomad Visa — English & Spanish

**How to use this:**
- Put it on your **company letterhead**, fill the bracketed fields, sign as the company's **legal representative/owner**, and have it **notarized and apostilled** (US Dept of State / state Secretary of State), then **translated into Spanish** if you submit the English version.
- If your company has a **co-owner, officer, or partner** other than you, it's stronger to have *them* sign it about you. If you're the sole owner, you sign as legal representative — that's accepted; back it with your incorporation docs and bank/merchant statements.
- Submit alongside: your **company registration/incorporation docs (apostilled)**, **bank statements** (showing the ~monthly income to you), and **merchant-account statements** (showing foreign-client source).

---

## ENGLISH

**[COMPANY LETTERHEAD]**

[Date]

**To:** Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Colombia (Ministerio de Relaciones Exteriores) — Visa Section
**Re:** Remote-work certification for [James Full Legal Name] — application for the Digital Nomad Visa (Visa V – Nómada Digital)

To whom it may concern,

I, [Signatory Full Name], acting in my capacity as [Owner / Managing Member / Legal Representative] of **[Company Name]**, hereby certify the following:

1. **[Company Name]** is a [e.g., limited liability company] duly organized and existing under the laws of the State of [State], United States of America (registration/EIN No. [____]), with its registered address at [company address] and website [website]. The company provides [brief description of services, e.g., "digital marketing and consulting services"] to clients located **outside of Colombia**.

2. **[James Full Legal Name]**, a citizen of the United States, holder of U.S. passport No. [passport number], is the [owner / a partner and shareholder] of [Company Name] and serves as [title, e.g., "Owner and Managing Member"].

3. In that role, Mr./Ms. [Last Name] performs his/her duties **entirely remotely**, by digital and telecommunications means, and is able to carry out his/her work from any location, **including from Colombia**. His/her work is performed exclusively for the company and its non-Colombian clients.

4. For these services, he/she receives compensation of approximately **US$[amount] per month** (paid as [salary / owner's distribution]), deposited to his/her account at [bank name]. This income has been consistent over at least the past [six] months and is expected to continue throughout his/her stay in Colombia.

5. This remote working arrangement is ongoing, and the company supports his/her relocation to Colombia while continuing to perform this work remotely. The company has **no commercial activity with Colombian clients** in connection with this role.

This letter is issued at the holder's request for the sole purpose of his/her Digital Nomad visa application before the Government of Colombia.

Sincerely,

_______________________________
[Signatory Full Name]
[Title], [Company Name]
[Email] · [Phone]
