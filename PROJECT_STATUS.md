## Core recommendations reached
### Money transfer (Melissa)
- **Wire USD (not pesos)** from Schwab to a Colombian bank (IMC); the bank converts and files the **declaración de cambio** classifying it as **inversión extranjera directa en inmuebles** → auto-registers the investment with Banco de la República (required for repatriation rights AND the visa).
- **Do NOT use Wise/Remitly** for a property purchase — they bypass the foreign-investment registration.
- Each installment must be channeled/registered separately; registration accrues per tranche.
- Negotiate the FX rate toward the TRM (each 1% ≈ $7K on $700K).
- Biggest cost is US **capital-gains timing** — installments naturally spread it across tax years (good).

### Structure
- **Hold the 6 rentals in a Colombian SAS** (liability shield, expense deductions, business vehicle). Personal-use house could be held personally.
- **The installment/no-title problem makes the SAS even more important:** the **Socio/Propietario visa is keyed to registered investment, not title**, so it sidesteps the "no title for years" problem. Alternative fix: take title now with a registered *hipoteca* to the seller.

### Visas
- **Melissa:** best = **R (Resident) investor visa** (she clears the ~650-SMMLV threshold) IF she can show title — otherwise **Visa M – Socio/Propietario** via the SAS (works without title). **Retirement (Pensionado) visa is the WRONG fit** — needs a lifetime pension, bans running a business, 5 yrs to residency. (Her current attorney suggested Pensionado; the letter challenges this.)
- **James:** **Visa M – Socio/Propietario** via Melissa's SAS is the realistic "move this summer + draw company income" path. (Employee work visa won't work — new SAS can't meet the 100-SMMLV employer-income test. Digital Nomad visa is fastest but bans Colombian-company income and doesn't count toward residency.)
- **Wife + daughter:** beneficiaries on James's visa (can study, **cannot work**). Wife's **Argentine passport → Mercosur visa** would give her **open work rights + residency in 2 years** — the family's best long-term anchor. Argentine passport timeline: ~6–12 months via US consulate (gated by appointment + ~3-mo DNI shipping); fastest if done in-country in Argentina via RENAPER.
- **Tax residency:** >183 days/yr in Colombia = taxed on worldwide income (up to 39%). Staying under 183 days keeps her taxed only on Colombian-source income. Visa ≠ tax residency.

### Rentals (long vs short term)
- 6 units = a business either way (ICA, RUT, e-invoicing).
- **Short-term/tourist:** higher yield but 19% IVA, RNT tourism registry, more admin.
- **Long-term residential:** IVA-exempt, simpler, lower yield, tenant-protective (Ley 820).
- **Zoning (POT/uso-de-suelo) is the #1 due-diligence risk** for mountainside cabins.
