1. Information we collect

Information you give us

When you register for a workshop or event, fill out a form, buy something, book a call or contact us, we may collect:

  • Your name (first and last)
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number, if you give it to us
  • Business information you choose to share, such as your industry, expertise and goals
  • Purchase and order details, such as what you bought, the amount, the date and your billing details
  • Anything else you choose to share through forms, surveys, calls or messages

Card payments are handled by our payment processor through GoHighLevel, our checkout platform. We do not store your full card number.

Information collected automatically

When you visit our pages, we may automatically collect:

  • Your IP address and approximate location
  • Your browser type, device type and operating system
  • The pages you visit, how long you stay and the page that sent you to us
  • Cookies and similar tracking tools (see section 5)
  • Campaign or referral details passed in the page link

2. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Register you for workshops and events and send you the access details and reminders
  • Process your orders and deliver what you bought
  • Book and run calls you schedule with us
  • Send you emails and, if you opted in, text messages about your registration, our programs, events and offers
  • Respond to your questions and give you support
  • Measure and improve our ads, pages and services
  • Keep our pages and checkout secure and prevent fraud
  • Meet our legal obligations

3. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business, and only as needed for them to do that work for us:

  • GoHighLevel, our CRM, forms, checkout, calendar, email and SMS platform
  • Payment processors, which process card payments made through our checkout
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram), for advertising and ad measurement
  • Google, for analytics and advertising
  • Zoom and aEvent, for workshop registration, reminders and delivery
  • Cloudflare and Netlify, which host our pages and files

We may also share information if the law, a court order or a government authority requires it, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of business assets.

4. Advertising, Meta Pixel and Conversions API

We use the Meta Pixel and the Meta Conversions API to measure how our ads perform and to show relevant ads. These tools tell Meta about actions on our pages, such as viewing a page, registering or buying. When you register or buy, we may send Meta your email address or phone number in hashed (scrambled) form so Meta can match the action to an ad. Meta's use of this information is covered by Meta's own privacy policy.

You can manage the ads you see in your Facebook or Instagram ad preferences settings.

5. Cookies and tracking

We and our service providers use cookies and similar tools to:

  • Keep your session and checkout working
  • Understand how people use our pages
  • Show and measure our ads

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some parts of our pages may not work as well if you do.

6. Text messages (SMS)

If you give us your mobile number and opt in, you agree to receive text messages from us about your registration and our programs. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, and reply HELP for help. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of any purchase.

We do not share mobile numbers or SMS opt in data with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.

7. Your choices

  • Email: click the unsubscribe link in any email from us, or email us and ask to be removed.
  • Text messages: reply STOP to any text.
  • Ads: change your ad preferences in your Facebook, Instagram or Google account settings, or block cookies in your browser.
  • Access or correction: email us to ask for a copy of the information we hold about you or to fix anything that is wrong.

8. How to request deletion of your data

Email james@soniagomez.tv with the subject "Delete my data". Please send it from the email address you used with us, or include that email address and your phone number in the message.

Within 30 days, we delete your contact record, your form answers, and your email, phone and SMS history in GoHighLevel, and we remove you from our Meta custom audiences. We confirm by email when it's done.

Order and payment records that we must keep by law are kept only as long as the law requires.

This also applies to any data connected to our Facebook or Instagram ads or apps.

9. Data retention

We keep your information only as long as we need it for the purposes in this policy, to keep our business records, to meet legal obligations, to resolve disputes and to enforce our agreements. You can ask us to delete your data at any time (see section 8).

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong
  • Ask us to delete your personal information
  • Opt out of marketing emails and texts at any time
  • Withdraw your consent where we rely on it

To use any of these rights, email us at james@soniagomez.tv.

11. California residents (CCPA)

If you live in California, the California Consumer Privacy Act gives you more rights, including the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to ask us to delete it, the right to opt out of the sale of personal information, and the right not to be treated differently for using these rights. We do not sell personal information.

12. Children's privacy

Our programs are for business professionals and are not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

13. Third party links

Our pages may link to other websites, including our related brands. We are not responsible for how other sites handle your information, so please read their privacy policies.
